# Change Record Template

Write for a CAB member who has never seen your system. If they can approve it on the first read, the record is good.

## 1. Summary
- **Title:** what is changing, in one line
- **Release type:** major / medium / minor / hotfix
- **Business reason:** why this change, why now
- **Linked items:** stories, defects, risk findings

## 2. Scope and impact
- **Systems and components affected:**
- **Users or clients affected:**
- **Expected downtime or degradation:** none / describe
- **Dependencies on other teams or changes:**

## 3. Risk assessment
- **Risk level:** low / medium / high, with one line on why
- **What could go wrong:**
- **How we reduce it:** for example feature toggle, phased or blue-green deployment, deploying in a quiet window

## 4. Testing evidence
- **Environments tested:**
- **Test results:** link to evidence
- **Open defects:** none / list with severity and agreed handling

## 6. Rollback plan
- **Trigger:** the specific condition that means we roll back
- **Steps:** numbered, with owners
- **Time to roll back:**
- **Rollback tested on:** date and environment

## 7. Verification
- **Post-implementation verification (PIV):** technical checks, owner and timing
- **Business PVT:** what the business confirms, tester and timing
